# 013. The name Tonnr

Status: decided by Téo on 2026-10-08, who bought `tonnr.app` that day.

## Context

The product was called Tonn, "wave" in Breton, in Irish, and in Scottish Gaelic. Téo wanted a name that is short, works abroad, and reads well for the app and for a public API, on a domain other than `.surf`.

Two products in Ireland are already called Tonn: a clothing label drawn from surfing, at `tonnsurf.com` since 2015, and a site of early-career jobs at `tonn.app`. Every short `tonn` domain is taken, and `tonn.surf` would sit one dot away from the clothing label.

Houl, "swell" in Breton, was the other candidate. An English speaker reads it as "howl", and a padel app launched under that name in December 2025, at `houl.app`.

## Decision

- The product is named Tonnr: tonn, the wave, with an r.
- Its domain is `tonnr.app`. The API is meant to live at `api.tonnr.app`.

## Consequences

- The name is not empty. `tonnr.co` is the site of a DJ who goes by TONNR, and `tonnr.com` was registered in December 2025 and shows a parking page.
- The GitHub account `tonnr` is taken. The npm package `tonnr` was free on 2026-10-08.
- No trademark register was searched. Do that before paying for a logo.
- The name has to be spelled out: tonn, r, no e.
- The repository is `teo-goulois/tonnr` on GitHub.
