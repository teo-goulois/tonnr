# 078. The forms of the app for phones

Status: decided on 2026-10-22, as the app for phones was drawn again to look and behave as the web's phone layout does, which Téo asked for that day after he tried a build on his iPhone. Decision 076 left open whether the app's forms take TanStack Form: they did not, and each of its forms kept its state by hand. Four of them greyed their button until the form was right and never said why, none gave the focus to a wrong field, and two components drew a field, each its own way.

## Decision

- A form of the app for phones is made with `useAppForm`, from `apps/native/src/components/ui/form.tsx`: TanStack Form, as on the web, with adapters of the app's own kit. Its fields are `field.Input`, `field.Textarea`, `field.Choice`, for a group of toggles, and `field.Control`, which binds a control the app draws itself, a rating, sizes among pictures, the photos of a session, and hands it its value, its name, its fault and a ref to set on what a screen reader is moved to. Its button is `form.SubmitButton`, and a field's return key sends it through `useFormSend`. TanStack Form is JavaScript alone: an update of the app's JavaScript carries it (decision 075).
- The rules are decision 076's. A form is checked by a Zod schema at `validators.onSubmit` and says nothing before it is sent. Once sent, each wrong field says why under itself, the first takes the focus, a field typed in the keyboard and any other a screen reader, and is scrolled to when it stands out of view in the form's `FormScroll`, and a field that becomes wrong shakes lightly, unless the reader asked for no motion. The phone also buzzes once for a send that was refused. The button is never disabled to stop a wrong form, only while nothing changed or a file still goes up. What the API refuses about a field is said under it with `refuseField`, and a refusal about nothing in particular is a toast, `useTell`.
- `Field`, in `input.tsx`, is the web's form field: its name over the control, what it says of itself under it, and its fault in the theme's red in that place, with what it said behind a small button. A screen reader hears the control by the field's name, with its fault or what it says as its hint, and iOS, which reads no live region, is told a new fault in words. `Input` writes at 16 points and draws the web's focus ring, whose color `pnpm --filter @repo/ui run native-theme` writes into the app's theme from `--focus-ring-outline`. `Textarea` is the same field for several lines. The adapters draw their fields with these, and a form that needs no form of TanStack's, a field and its button on one line, uses them alone.
- A form sheet has the web drawer's head, `PanelHeader`: a title at the start, a line under it, a chevron back when there is a step to go back to, and the cross that closes it. What the form sends stands at its foot, `SheetFooter`, or at the end of what scrolls, as on the web, and never in its head.
- The app asks before a sheet that holds something written is left, with `useSheetGuard`, where the web asks only of the alert editor: a sheet of the platform is swiped away more easily than the web's drawer is closed. The alert editor asks in the web's words.
- The alert editor keeps its draft in `packages/client/src/alerts/alert-words.ts`, as decision 076 says of both apps: its name is a field that writes the draft, and its sentence the field of the conditions.
- `.oxlintrc.json` refuses an import of `@tanstack/react-form` other than a type in `apps/native`, outside the kit, with this decision's number. The design system shows the kit's two sample forms, the web's, one of them sent as it opens.

## Consequences

- A button that stayed grey on the phone says why under a field once pressed, as on the web.
- The second field the app had, `components/auth/field.tsx`, is gone: every form of the app is drawn with the kit's.
