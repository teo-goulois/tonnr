## A header that floats on the map

On a wide screen the header no longer takes a band over the map: it floats on it, as the map's own buttons do. On the left, the name and a search field that opens with a press or with ⌘K. On the right, the saved places, the alerts and the account: each opens its panel, and a second press closes it. The button that switched the theme is gone from there: the theme is chosen in the account's settings, and the key that switches it still works.
