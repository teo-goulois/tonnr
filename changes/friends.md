## Friends, by a link of your own

Your account now has a link of its own, in the account's panel under "Friends": send it, or show its code to someone on the car park. Whoever creates an account through it has you as sponsor, and you are friends. Someone who already has an account opens it and becomes your friend.

Friends read each other's username, rank and what each wrote in the guides. Nothing of your spots, your alerts or your lists, unless you share a list by its own link. Either of you ends the friendship when they like.

An account you brought counts for your score once it has had a contribution of its own confirmed.
