## Find a spot by its name

On a phone, the magnifier among the map's buttons opens a search: type the name of a spot, of a buoy or of a town, and press what it finds to open it. Your own spots come first, then the surf breaks nearest where the map is. A name is found with or without its accents: "guethary" finds Guéthary. Before you type, the search lists what you looked at last.

On a wide screen the same button opens the command palette, which now finds your spots and the surf breaks by their name.
