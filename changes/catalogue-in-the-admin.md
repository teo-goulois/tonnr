## The catalogue of spots, kept from the admin

The admin has a page Catalogue. Every spot of the catalogue is on a map: open one to change its name, move its point, say what it is like and write a short description, which the spot's guide then shows. Two spots that are the same place are merged into one, keeping what each knew, and a spot nobody uses can be deleted. Spots less than 60 m apart are listed, to find the ones told twice.

Its tab Import goes through a list of spots one at a time, or finds one by its name or its place, with what the list says of each, its pages and the page of its map, the spots around it and the others it may be, and a search of any spot by its name: add it, merge it into a spot that is there already, put it off or leave it out.
