## Add your own spots to the map

The pin with a plus on the map, or the "+" at the bottom of a phone, adds a spot of your own. Search for a beach or a town, or press the map where the wave breaks: a marker goes there, which you can drag, or move with the arrow keys. Give the spot a name and save it. It then stands on the map as a pin, its panel shows the forecast and the tide at that point, and it is listed under "My spots" in your saved places. A spot is yours alone until you say otherwise. Alerts on a spot are being redesigned and are not set from here yet.
