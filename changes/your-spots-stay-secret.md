## The landing says what becomes of a secret spot

Under the cards of what the product does, the landing now says in four lines who can read your spots: no other account, key or public page; they leave only by a list you share, a request to make one public or an assistant you connect; the statistics carry neither their name nor their position; and the code is open, so each rule can be checked. Beside the rules, a spot of your own stands on the map as the app draws it, with the line its panel says: private, seen by you alone.
