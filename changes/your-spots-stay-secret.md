## The landing says what becomes of a secret spot

Under the cards of what the product does, the landing now says what the app keeps of your spots and who can read them: no other account, key or public page reads a spot of yours; it leaves only by a list you share, a request to make it public or an assistant you connect; the usage statistics carry neither its name nor its position; and the code is open, so every one of these rules can be checked. The same section says the one limit, as the privacy policy does: whoever runs the server can read its database, to keep the service running.
