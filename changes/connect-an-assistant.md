## Let your assistant read your spots, alerts and sessions

Add Tonnr to Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant by its address, sign in, and allow it: it then answers from your own spots, the alerts you set on them and your log of sessions, with the forecast and the tide beside them. It reads and changes nothing, it is not told your email address, and you disconnect it at any time from your account.
