## Your measurements in your units

Choose how each measurement is written: the wind in knots, km/h, m/s or mph, the heights in metres or feet, the temperatures in °C or °F, the distances in kilometres or miles, and the rain in millimetres or inches. The units are in your account's settings. Every screen follows at once: the map's badges and its legends, the panels, the curves, the tide, the week's table and the sentence of an alert. Your account keeps the choice, so it is the same on your phone.
