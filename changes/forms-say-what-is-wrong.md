## Forms say what's wrong

Every form of the app now works the same way. A button no longer stays grey until a form is right: press it, and each field that's wrong says why under itself, in your language, and the first one takes the focus. Type, and the message goes. That covers signing up and in, your username, a new spot and its name, a list, a shared list's password, an alert, a session, a report, a guide, your profile, deleting your account, and the forms that send an idea or a message.

When the server refuses something about a field, a username that's taken or a wrong password, it's said under that field rather than in a passing notice. An alert that asks nothing yet says "Pick at least one condition." under its sentence.

The admin's forms follow the same rules.
