## A panel that shows less, or more

The panel of a spot, a surf break or a buoy now shows as much of the forecast as you ask. "Essentials" says the sea now in one sentence, in the words of your alerts, tells you what fits the spot and what does not, and lists the days ahead with the wind named by how it meets the shore. "Details" is the panel as it was, without the energy, the second swell, the gusts and the model's figures beside the buoy's. "Everything" is all of it. Choose in the account's settings, or press "Show less" and "Show more" at the end of the panel: your account keeps the choice on every device.
