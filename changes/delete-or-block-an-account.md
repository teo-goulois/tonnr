## Delete your account, or block another

The last row of your account's panel deletes your account, at once: give your password again, or sign in again if you sign in with Google. Your spots, lists, alerts, sessions, reports and photos go with it, and what you wrote in a guide that was confirmed stays, under no name. Nobody can take your username for 30 days. The privacy policy says how at `/privacy#delete`.

You can also block an account from a report's menu, from the name of whoever wrote a line of a guide, or from a friend. You then stop seeing its reports, what it suggests in guides before it's confirmed, and its username; a friendship between you ends, and no link makes another. The account isn't told. The accounts you blocked are listed in your account's panel, where you can unblock them.
