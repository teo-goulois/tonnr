## The swell alone, beside the waves

The waves a forecast gives are the whole sea: the swell that comes from far away, and the chop the wind raises on the spot. The week's table of a spot now has a row "Swell" under the waves, with where it comes from: the swell alone, as the model tells it apart. It is less sure than the waves, and the credits say which model it comes from.

An alert has an advanced part that asks about that swell: "In those waves, the swell alone is 0.8 m or more, from W to NW". It says what the two are at your spot now, so you can see when they differ.
