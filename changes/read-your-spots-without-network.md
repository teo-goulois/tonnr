## Read a buoy or a spot where there's no network

The phone app keeps what it last read of a place: the forecast, the buoy's readings, the tide and the guide, the map's buoys and breaks, and your spots and lists. Check your spot at home, and it still shows at the beach with no signal, with a line saying when it was updated. Newer data replaces it as soon as the network is back.
