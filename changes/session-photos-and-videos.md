## Photos and videos in your sessions

A session of your log now keeps your photos and videos: up to ten, a minute of video each. Pick them in the session's form, and each one goes up while you fill in the rest; a session saved while a video still goes up keeps it once it arrives. The log shows the first one beside each session, and a press opens them over the whole screen, one after the other.

They are yours alone. Each file is made smaller on arrival, keeps its quality, and loses what it said of where, when and with what it was taken. An account keeps 2 GB of them, and they go with their session.
