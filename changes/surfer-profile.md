## Say what kind of surfer you are

When you open the map with your account, a few questions come up once: your level, the waves you surf from the smallest to the biggest, picked among pictures of a surfer beside a wave, how much of a forecast you read, and your boards. Each one can be skipped, and "Later" puts them all off. Your answer on reading a forecast sets how much the panels show. You change your answers later under "Your surfing" in your account. An assistant you signed in, Claude or ChatGPT, reads them to fit its advice to you.
