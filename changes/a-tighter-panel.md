## A tighter panel for a buoy

The curves of a buoy now show 12, 24 or 48 hours on each side of now, or the week, and open on 24 hours. Their figures stand in columns under one legend, so the buoy and the model read down the four curves. The week's table has a button that hides the names of its rows, to give the hours more room once you know them. The panel also wastes less space between its parts, and the tip on how to read a curve with a finger goes away once you have done it.
