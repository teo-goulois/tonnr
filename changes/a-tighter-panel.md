## A tighter panel for a buoy

The curves of a buoy now show 12, 24 or 48 hours on each side of now, or the week, and open on 24 hours. Their figures stand in columns, so the buoy and the model read down the four curves, and the legend is behind the info button. An arrow over the names of the week's table folds them to their first letter, to give the hours more room once you know the rows. The panel also wastes less space between its parts, and the tip on how to read a curve with a finger goes away once you have done it.
