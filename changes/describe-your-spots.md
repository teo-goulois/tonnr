## Describe your spots

A spot of your own now has a guide, as a surf break of the catalogue has. Right after you save a spot, or later from its "Guide" tab, press the words that apply: the kind of break, the wave, the bottom, the level, the boards, the season, the tide, and where the swell and the wind should come from. Nothing is required, and you can change it whenever you like.
