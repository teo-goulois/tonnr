## How big the waves get here, against the forecast

Two breaks can read the same forecast and not be the same size. The panel of a surf break now says, under its tiles, how many times the forecast its waves usually run, learned from the sizes said in everyone's sessions there, once five sessions from two accounts gave one, and by the swell of the hour when enough of them were in it. The guide gains a line for it, "Size against the forecast", in five steps from about half to more than double, with one sentence on why. The tiles, the charts and the alerts stay in the forecast's units: the sentence is an estimate, and says where it comes from.
