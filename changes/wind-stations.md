## Wind stations worth opening

A station that measures the wind alone has a panel of its own: the wind now, its gusts and where it comes from, then a curve of the last 6 hours, 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days. The model's wind is drawn beside it as a dashed line that goes on past now, so you see what the wind did and what is forecast next.

On a surf break or a spot of your own, the wind is read on the nearest station that measured it lately, with how it meets the shore there: offshore, cross-shore or onshore. Press it to open the station.

Météo-France's stations are among them on an instance that holds a key: about 780 across France, a reading every six minutes.

A direction is now always written with its arrow.
