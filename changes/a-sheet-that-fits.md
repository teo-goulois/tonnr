## A panel that fits what it shows on a phone

On a phone, the panel of a buoy, a break or a spot now opens just tall enough for its figures of the moment, with nothing cut under them. Press its handle or its name, or slide it up, to read the rest. A place you choose from a search, your saved places or a link opens fully at once. The map's buttons no longer stay in the middle of the screen after the panel is closed.
