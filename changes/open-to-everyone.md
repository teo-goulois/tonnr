## Open the map without an account

The map now opens for everyone: the buoys, the wind stations, the surf breaks, the forecasts and the tides, with nothing to sign up for. An account is for what you keep: your spots, your lists and your alerts. The app asks for one when you save something.

When a lot of people come at once, the app may ask you to sign in or to create an account before it shows the map. A list someone shared with you by a link opens either way.
