## A front page that shows the map, and the alerts it sends

The top of the front page now stands on the map itself, which moves a little with your pointer. On a wide screen, alerts fall over it one after another, as they would on your phone. The picture of the app that you could start in the page is gone. The map comes in out of a blur, and sinks behind the words as you scroll. Each part of the page below comes in as you reach it: its title first, then what it shows, and the alert's sentence is marked part by part, as its editor marks what you set.
