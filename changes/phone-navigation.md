## A phone's own navigation

On a phone the header is gone. A bar at the bottom leads to the map, your saved places, your alerts and your account, each a whole screen, and the map now reaches the top. The "+" in the middle of the bar adds a spot or an alert: for an alert, say which spot it is for, and its panel opens on a new one.

Your lists are on the map itself, behind the button under the one that finds you: press it, then a list to see it alone there, and "All" to see everything again.

Your account has a screen with its settings: the units, how to be told of your alerts, the theme and the language. On a wide screen it opens from your account's menu, under "Settings". The alert settings moved there from the alerts panel, which has a button that leads to them.
