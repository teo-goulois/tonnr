## Learn to read a surf forecast

Three guides under "Learn", on the landing and beside the map: the basics before a first session, how to read a week and find the right window, and what the figures are made of. Each lesson has a demo you move yourself, drawn with the same pieces as the app, and ends on one line to remember. Every technical word opens a glossary entry that says what the word means and where the thing comes from. A figure in a demo is written in the unit you pick beside it, which the app then uses everywhere. On the page of a spot, each underlined part of the sentence that says the sea now leads to the lesson that explains it.
