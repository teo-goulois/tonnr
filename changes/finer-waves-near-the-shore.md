## A finer wave model from Brittany to the North Sea

From the French coast north of La Rochelle to Ireland, Great Britain and the North Sea, the waves of a forecast now come from a model with a grid of 1.5 km, where they came from one of 9 km. Near the shore it was right more often against the buoys, and it sees the shelter of a bay or a headland that the coarser model does not.

It reaches about six days ahead. The days after come from the same model as before, and the credits of a panel say when one takes over from the other.
