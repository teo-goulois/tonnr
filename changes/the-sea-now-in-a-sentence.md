## What the sea does now, in a sentence, on a spot's page

The page of a surf spot now opens with one sentence: the waves, their period and where they come from, the wind and how it meets the shore, and the tide, rising or falling, with the next high or low water. Under it, what fits the spot and what does not, judged by what the spot itself likes: the swell's direction, the size, the wind and the tide. A button sets an alert at the spot: the map opens on it, and if the spot is not one of yours yet, it becomes one first.
