## The waves right now, sent from the beach

Each surf break now has a Reports tab, right after the forecast: photos and clips of the waves that surfers sent from there in the last week, the last few hours first, with how big the waves looked and a few words. The break's public page shows them too.

Send one from the tab, with up to four photos or clips. Before it shows, each one is checked: shot within 2 km of the break, in the two hours before you sent it, and showing the waves. The place and time come from the file, or from where your phone is when you send, since an iPhone's browser strips them; the check of what a photo shows runs on our own server, and the files lose what they said of where and when they were shot. If yours doesn't pass, you see why. Anyone can flag a report, and three flags hide it until a moderator decides.
