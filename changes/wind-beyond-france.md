## Wind beyond France

The map reads the wind from four more networks, none of which needs a key. Denmark, the Faroes and Greenland come from DMI, with gusts. The Netherlands come from Rijkswaterstaat: the coast, the Wadden and the platforms of the North Sea. Japan comes from AMeDAS: about 900 stations, many on capes. The United States come from NOAA's tide stations: about 230 sensors on piers and harbours, with gusts, which the buoys' network did not carry.

Where several stations stand within 2 km of each other, the map draws one: the one that measured in the last two hours, then the one that gives gusts, then the one that measures most often. If it stops, its neighbour takes its place. Every station is still kept, inland ones too, and the API gives them all unless asked for one by place.
