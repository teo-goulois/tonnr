## Read a buoy beside the model, and the week as a grid

The panel of a buoy now shows the swell, its period, its energy and the wind one under the other, on the same span of time, with what the buoy measured drawn over what the model computed. Below, the week ahead is a grid with a column for each hour, with the clouds, the rain and the air.

A surf break has the same grid, a tab for what is known of the break, and the nearest buoy that still reports: it opens over the break, and closing it gives the break back.
