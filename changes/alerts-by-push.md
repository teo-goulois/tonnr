## Alerts on your phone, by push

Switch push on for a device in your alert settings, and what your alerts tell reaches it as a notification: a window that takes shape, one that moved or went, the evening before, and when it begins. A notification gives the hours of the first spots, in your time and units. Press it and the app opens on the spot, or on your alerts when it speaks of several. What is said again of the same windows takes the place of what was said before, so your screen shows where they stand.

The switch is "On this device", in the alert settings of your account: each browser you switch it on in is one of your devices. They are listed under the switch, and any of them can be removed from any other. On an iPhone or an iPad, add Tonnr to your Home Screen first. Signing out stops the notifications on that device.

Never during your quiet hours, 24 a day at most, and a phone that was off is not told of a window that is over. What a notification says is encrypted for your browser: the service that carries it reads none of it.
