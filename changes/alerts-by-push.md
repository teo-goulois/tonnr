## Alerts on your phone, by push

Switch push on for a device in your alert settings, and what your alerts tell reaches it as a notification: a window that takes shape, one that moved or went, the evening before, and when it begins. A notification gives the hours of the first spots, in your time and units, and opens the spot when you press it. What is said of a window that moved takes the place of what was said before, so your screen shows where the window stands.

Never during your quiet hours, 24 a day at most, and a phone that was off is not told of a window that is over. What a notification says is encrypted for your browser: the service that carries it reads none of it.
