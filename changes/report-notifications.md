## Know when someone shows the waves at your spots

When someone sends a report at a spot you saved in a list, you hear about it: a notification on the devices where you turned push on, with the first photo where your phone shows one, and a line in the alerts panel. Several reports at one spot within three hours make one notification, and you get six at most a day, never during your quiet hours. A tap opens the spot on its reports.

Turn them off for all your spots in your notification settings, or for one list from the list itself.

When you send a report, you now see how many people saw it, opened its photos, and were told about it. Only you see these numbers, and they name nobody.
