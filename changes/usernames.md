## A username for your account

An account now has a username: the name other people read, where Tonnr shows who wrote something. Creating an account asks for one in place of a name, and tells you whether it is free as you type. It is three to twenty letters, digits or underscores, kept in small letters.

If you made your account before, nothing of it changed and your name stays yours alone. You choose a username in the account's panel, and change it there whenever you like.
