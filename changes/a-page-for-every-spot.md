## A page for every surf spot

Every surf spot of the catalogue has a page of its own, at `/spots/<country>/<region>/<spot>`, in the four languages: what the spot is like, what surfers say of it, the sea as the nearest buoy and wind station measure it, the next tides, the week's forecast and the spots nearby, with a way to the map. The countries and the regions have pages that list their spots. Search engines are asked to list the spots the catalogue says enough of.
