## Keep a log of your sessions

After a surf, add a session: on a phone from the "+" beside the bar, and on any screen from the "Sessions" tab of a surf break or of one of your spots. Say when it began, how long it lasted, how it was from one to five stars, and a few words if you like. You can write it later: only the day and the hour matter.

What the sea did is kept with each session: the waves, the wind, the tide, the air, and what the nearest buoy measured. For a session of the last two days that includes the swell apart from the wind sea. Further back, up to 90 days, it is the whole sea and the wind, and beyond that the tide alone.

Once three sessions at a place are rated four stars or more, its "Sessions" tab says what you like there: the heights, the periods, the directions and the tides your best sessions had in common. At one of your own spots, one press makes an alert of it.

Your logbook is in your account: how many sessions and how long in the water this month and this year, where you surf most, and every session month by month. Nobody else reads it.
