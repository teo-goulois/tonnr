## An instance that keeps up

Before the release, what the service asks of its providers was audited, and what could make it ask too much was fixed. One person, one shared list or one visitor may now ask the forecasts a few hundred times an hour: enough for a day of looking at spots, not enough to spend the instance's day on everyone else. The wave models of Copernicus Marine are read once the forecast provider has answered, shared between the points that need the same piece, and left alone for a minute when they fail. A forecast the provider answered too short is asked again after ten minutes, not at every look, and a provider that asks to be left alone is. A forecast asked for at a pole no longer holds the API.

The web app also asks who is signed in once a minute instead of at every press on the map, and the admin's overview starts with the instance in a glance: accounts, calls, forecast budget, visitors and worker.
